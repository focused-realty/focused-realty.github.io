Why here

Six things that move the needle

The future is already being built here

Sofrim Compound is Bnei Brak's most innovative new project: an entire neighborhood rising from the ground at a standard the city has not seen before. Come in today and you come in at the start of the story.

Close to the business hubs — far from the noise

Minutes from the Ramat Gan Diamond Exchange, the B.S.R business towers and Petah Tikva's tech district — and still, the quiet of a neighborhood.

On the banks of the Yarkon

A grand promenade beside the neighborhood, a major park in advanced stages of development, and open views over green space. Front-row nature, in the heart of Greater Tel Aviv.

Community, country club, study halls

Strong communities already taking root, synagogues and study halls inside the compound, and a neighborhood country club on the way. A complete life, within walking distance.

You feel it the moment you walk in

An entrance that greets you on another level, a high-end specification, and a private storage room with every apartment. This is the innovative Bnei Brak, and here they build differently.

Pay less. Get more.

Attractive entry prices relative to everything around — and upside that grows with every stage of the compound's development.
